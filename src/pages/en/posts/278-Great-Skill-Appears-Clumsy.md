## Trending Tools

**AgentsView: browse and analyze how you use agents**
<https://www.agentsview.io/>
A local-first app for browsing, searching, and analyzing your past AI coding sessions. It shows where your agents spend their time and money, with separate views by project, model, and tool.
<img src="https://cdn.tw93.fun/uPic/uYtRtK52.png" width="800" />

**Discover startup product launch videos from X**
<https://whatships.com/>
Ding built a tool that collects product launch videos currently being shared on X. Quite a few are very well made and worth learning from. Next time you have a product to launch, take a look and see if any of them spark a new idea.
<img src="https://cdn.tw93.fun/uPic/ibxdqH09.png" width="800" />

**Give your AI a brain.md**
<https://projectbrain.md/>
The idea is interesting. The author says AI forgets everything unless you give it a brain, and brain.md is that brain.
<img src="https://cdn.fliggy.com/pic/9RcLFg00.png" width="800" />

**nowdex: quickly check your agent usage**
<https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/nowdex/id6791450777>
A free iOS app that lets you quickly check your agent usage, including from a Home Screen widget.
<img src="https://cdn.tw93.fun/uPic/ysicE547.png" width="800" />

**How to remove the annoying Claude Code URL Handler.app**
I finally got rid of the Claude Code URL Handler.app that Claude Code registers on macOS. It has no icon and looks ugly, which had been driving the neat freak in me crazy, and uninstalling it directly didn't help. Add `"disableDeepLinkRegistration": "disable"` to `~/.claude/settings.json` and it will stop coming back.
<img src="https://cdn.fliggy.com/pic/mEPOJg41.png" width="800" />
